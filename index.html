Today's Featured Drill

Logical Reasoning · Necessary Assumption

Stimulus. Kris Jenner argues that the family should film the next season in Calabasas rather than Aspen. She says: "Every season we've filmed in Calabasas, ratings went up. Therefore, filming in Calabasas causes ratings to go up."

Question. Which of the following is an assumption Kris's argument requires?

  1. Aspen seasons had lower ratings than Calabasas seasons.
  2. Kim is more popular than Kourtney.
  3. No other factor present during the Calabasas seasons better explains the increase in ratings.
  4. Filming in Calabasas is cheaper than filming in Aspen.
  5. The audience prefers warm-weather settings.

Answer: C.

Kris is making a classic correlation-to-causation leap. For her argument to hold, she has to assume nothing else — a new cast member, a viral moment, a competing show ending — is the real driver behind the ratings bump. Choice C is the necessary assumption: negate it ("there is another factor that better explains the bump"), and the argument collapses. That's the negation test in action.
